About Us

Welcome to the Photonics & Quantum Technology Lab at PES University. Our research focuses on cutting-edge developments in quantum computing, photonics, and VLSI architectures. Our lab strives to explore new frontiers in quantum cryptography, quantum machine learning, and multicore quantum processors.

Research Focus

Our lab's research spans quantum computing, quantum multicore architectures, and quantum/post-quantum cryptography. Our current projects include communication protocols for superconducting qubit-based processors, quantum cryptography schemes, and exploring symbolic dynamics in sequence analysis.
